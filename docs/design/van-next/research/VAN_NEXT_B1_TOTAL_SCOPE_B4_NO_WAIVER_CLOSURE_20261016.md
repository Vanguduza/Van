## 3. Replacement freeze semantics and automatic failure policy

The **design freeze** may approve visual/product intention and the mandatory full coverage contract; it does not complete B4 obligations. The programme is not **predevelopment ready** until the independent F15 reviewer verifies every before-F15 obligation, including a complete feature/action/product-truth preservation proof. It is not **release ready** until every implemented feature and applicable physical/financial outcome has passed later gates.

The five P2 slices never override mandatory P3/P4 coverage. All approved feature and action identities remain in the lock or have owner-signed supersessions. An implementation packet cannot close on subset feature success while an admitted sibling capability in its exit contract is missing.

Failure cases that must stop promotion: removed owner-action mapping, uninspected PR-only feature, missing Product Truth binding, omitted client caller, unexercised effect result, fake success receipt, unsupported broker capability displayed enabled, visual drift from dark or light locked goldens, or failure to verify real S24 interaction where required.

**Owner-controlled choices remain owner controlled:** final approval of product scope and visual themes, signed authority grants, choice of real payment provider, live risk/trading parameters and final device acceptance. All investigation, remediation, integration and test evidence stays an engineering responsibility.
