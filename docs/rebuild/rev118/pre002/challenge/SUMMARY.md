## Method and calibration

Identities linked to a requirement are the cited ones, plus registry siblings of a cited stem (`-POS/-NEG/-REC/-SEC/-INT`, `-P/-N`) and identities whose `requirement_id` names it. A negative or recovery case counts when a linked identity has a negative suffix, the acceptance has adversarial/recovery clauses, or a linked expectation states one (for example 'fails on an induced omission', 'no silent rounding').
Calibration against sampled records: cited-suffix-only detection flagged 205 requirements; linking siblings and `requirement_id` reduced it to 55; reading negative statements in expectations reduced it to 43. Every remaining case was checked by sample: the only linked identity is a one-line expectation such as 'Five working destinations'. These are heuristics; a flag is a question for the reviewer, and no flag is not a pass.


Requirements with no mechanical flag: 13. They still carry the five common questions; no flag means only that these checks found nothing.
