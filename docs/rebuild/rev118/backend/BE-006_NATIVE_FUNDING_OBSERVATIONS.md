# Native funding observations and honest owner performance

This is a partial BE-006 source component, not completion or acceptance of the funding programme. The original 131 units, 324 requirements, 1,610 acceptance identities, 234 contexts and 349 actions remain unchanged. BE-007 effects are not implemented or activated by this component.

The cTrader adapter reads the vendored native cash-history protocol in closed windows of at most one week. Native provider, demo/live realm, account ID, deposit asset and currency bind every statement. Row money precision, optional equity and balance version, negative amounts and unknown operation codes are preserved. Invalid or conflicting duplicate rows reject the whole statement. Account registration bounds are honored; no USD or missing-data zero is guessed. Free-text provider notes are excluded.

The existing append-only SQLite/PostgreSQL ledger stores original observations, first availability and statement identities. Retry, relabeling, concurrent collection and lost commit acknowledgement return the original receipt. Changed financial facts with the same native ID are retained as conflicts; they do not overwrite original history or its older as-of views. Classifications explicitly distinguish external capital, restricted credit, broker support, account costs/income and separate IB/mirroring business income. Classification does not establish trading alpha, settlement or payment approval.

Both existing account and single-session snapshot producers queue a bounded read worker. It uses its own authenticated native connection and its own connection to the original ledger, preserving named PostgreSQL scopes even for keyword connection strings. A slow history query does not execute on the order/protection socket or block snapshot submission. Pending jobs coalesce, failed windows retain their original cursor and weekly catch-up does not jump gaps. Runtime shutdown and failed account startup stop admission to the worker; a blocked daemon owns only its independent read connection and original observations, never a replacement payment operation.

The exact unitization core removes verified external deposits and withdrawals by issuing/redeeming rational units. Its source counterexamples cover deposits after losses, withdrawals after gains, full redemption/redeposit, credit already excluded by the normalizer, fees, missing valuations, ambiguous same-time order and resource bounds. Inputs must already be scoped, complete, ordered, normalized net equity. This pure calculator does not supply those facts or issue a provider qualification.

Historical cTrader cash rows contain total equity but do not supply the full contemporaneous restricted-credit valuation. Current native marks expose separately reported bonuses and calculate balance plus native net unrealized PnL without adding those bonuses. This does not justify assuming that historical total equity has the same basis. Native return qualification therefore stays `NOT_ISSUED`; unqualified day/week PnL and performance drawdown are null. The old raw equity differences remain separately labeled. Current-profile native account/currency/realm mismatches hide stale balances and cash facts. Missing or partial totals do not become zero performance.

Existing authenticated `/v1/trading/accounts` and `/v1/trading/portfolio` routes expose additive funding facts. Android `AccountCard` parses them with exact provider ID and money strings, including unknown/unavailable/conflict states and truncation metadata. No UI redesign or financial effect route is added. See [the owner read contract](FUNDING_OWNER_READ_CONTRACT.md).

Evidence is in `../receipts/NATIVE_FUNDING_OBSERVATIONS_SOURCE_20261024.json`: 103 native/accounting/runtime checks, 27 real PostgreSQL checks, 182 gateway/storage/backup integrations with no audited writes to source data, 19 registry plus 16 owner-runtime contracts, 80 rebuild checks, and five Android parser tests pass with no skips. These suites overlap and are not a unique-test total. All 12 final source-fault cases fail by behavioral assertion after passing controls; original source is restored. Earlier fixture/anchor failures and the real PostgreSQL worker defect are retained separately.

Remaining BE-006 work includes source-bound historical net-equity/credit normalization, complete balance-version/valuation ordering and reconciliation, other providers, corrections/reversals, consolidated transfers, funding reservations and risk-capital integration. BE-007 still requires its full quote/beneficiary/approval/reserve/submit/reconcile/settle/compensate and origin-bound hosted-handoff paths. No signed policy, withdrawal authority, F15/BG certificate, provider/live/deployment/security/recovery, handset or profitability qualification is issued.
