# Sampled flow-adjusted calculation

Status: source component; BE-006 remains IN_PROGRESS. A calculation result is not native source qualification, collateral, settlement or permission to trade.

The immutable `FlowAdjustedRisk` model combines the existing exact unitization kernel with intermediate normalized equity samples. Samples update sampled peaks and troughs without issuing or redeeming units. External capital retains its separate unit issuance/redemption; internal fees remain account losses and already-excluded credit does not become external capital. Full redemption and replacement capital retain prior sampled loss history. Missing normalized flow values or unclassified movements leave the full result UNMEASURED while preserving its known prefix, rather than publishing zero drawdown.

The independent fixture oracle starts with 100 units, deposits 100 at NAV 0.8 to reach 225 units, later withdraws 90 at NAV 1.2 to retain 150 units, and closes at NAV 1. An intermediate equity of 50 gives NAV 2/9 and sampled drawdown 7/9. Observing only the flow boundaries misses that trough and reports 0.2. The new model retains the intermediate observation without misclassifying either capital movement as trading PnL. These are normalized source-calculation fixtures, not evidence of live profitability.

Inputs require exact scoped typed points, original reference identities and integer clocks/order. A source reference cannot be reused for different input roles, and more than 4,096 flow/sample points refuse explicitly. Source IDs never supply chronological ordering. Conflicting equal-time values without native ordering remain unknown. Identical boundary or duplicate sample states remain auditable through `source_refs` and `redundant_sample_refs` while contributing no second economic point. Float values cannot hide inside a redundant sample. Private immutable serialization and the existing fixed report renderer preserve exact inputs and deterministic output under unrelated Decimal settings.

The actual native period report now constructs the model from independently validated original valuation snapshots. It retains the legacy performance view and its full source/coverage references while exposing the separate immutable calculation. Intermediate sampled drawdown comes from the shared calculator. Model capacity or source failure leaves an explicit unmeasured period. Native external-flow periods still stop at NORMALIZED_HISTORICAL_FLOW_EQUITY_UNAVAILABLE; this packet does not infer normalized historical equity from total broker equity, current bonuses or a numerical clock coincidence. Existing FLOW_ADJUSTED_RISK_NOT_QUALIFIED protection remains in force.

The model always reports native-source and settlement qualification as NOT_ISSUED, risk-capital qualification false and execution authority false. Its factory cannot independently certify caller-provided source facts. Actual ledger/provider/current-owner qualification remains with those existing owners.

The final source selection passed 394 checks with SQLite and actual isolated PostgreSQL exercised, no skips or storage-audit violations, matching final runtime/test hashes and independently confirmed fixture removal. Seven supplemental faults were caught by assertions, with ten positive and ten restored SQLite controls and exact source restoration. Supplemental PostgreSQL parameters were deselected only for that fault exercise and executed in the final actual-PostgreSQL selection. Counts overlap. Receipts and raw evidence belong to `receipts/SAMPLED_FLOW_RISK_SOURCE_20261026.json` and `ci/sampled_flow_risk_20261026/`.

Native source clock/normalization qualification, full multi-asset accounting, shared reservations, compensating fills/fees/reversals and BE-007 native payment rails remain open. This component does not complete the unit, independent/backend programme or Android/device qualification, and does not guarantee profitability. No frontend redesign precedes BG-001.
