# Current mandate validity at dispatch

Candidate source work for BE-002 and BE-009. Four valid counterexamples showed that a future-signed policy could approve present risk, an existing approval could reach the paper sender before its validity start or at expiry, and a policy expiring during native submission-guard acquisition still reached the sender.

The RiskAuthority now refuses a snapshot before the standing policy's signed validity start. The router checks the whole validity interval at entry and again after acquiring native submission authority, before consuming idempotency or publishing an order command. Actual single-symbol and account-book factories supply their current clock. Isolated replay callers retain their explicit event-clock argument. A missing, boolean or backward observed clock refuses new entry. The actual observed dispatch time reaches the adapter and native command, while its original decision time remains distinct.

Tests preserve the first valid and final valid millisecond; a pre-effect clock refusal does not consume the intent. Protective close/reduction remains available after expiry. Native factory tests reach a genuine strategy submission or real account-book route/lease using the private PostgreSQL fixture, then advance the clock after acquiring authority. Neither can send an expired entry. Removing either actual factory's clock is detected by those behavioural tests.

Existing test chronology needed correction: historical prices, calendar, strategy and assertions remain unchanged, while genuine test-owner grants now cover that exact historical study window. Current-time execution fixtures use their actual decision epoch instead of Unix-epoch toy values. A shared PostgreSQL test initially reused an account alias at two incompatible virtual times; the new book fixture now has its own registered, genuinely signed account identity. It preserves all other leases and epochs. An initial assertion referenced a nonexistent receipt field, and the first book fixture omitted its required second instrument; those oracle/setup failures are retained, not counted as product defects.

Final execution, actual deliberate faults and source restoration are in [the receipt](../receipts/MANDATE_DISPATCH_VALIDITY_SOURCE_20261022.json). The full source-corpus, original requirements/actions/units and frozen blueprint remain unchanged. No Android/new frontend, whole-unit, native provider, independent, production or S24 acceptance is issued.

This is the router's actual submission boundary. Deferred provider commands still require their own bounded envelope, original-result reconciliation and expiry/epoch checks before provider execution; a timeout or empty position snapshot is not NoEffect. Longer historical research studies must preserve their complete data horizon while distinguishing a frozen policy study from current financial authority. Those remain original BE-009/BE-012 obligations, alongside financial finality, persistent simulation, physical identity and safe retirement.
