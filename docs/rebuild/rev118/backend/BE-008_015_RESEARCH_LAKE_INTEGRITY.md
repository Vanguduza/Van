# Exact research-lake bytes, identities and concurrent publication

The existing bar lake now validates each instrument, timeframe, finite OHLC/volume/spread value, interval, duplicate and manifest claim before accepting research data. Conflicting overlapping observations require reconciliation rather than silently selecting the last value. This preserves uncertainty instead of allowing an arbitrary filesystem iteration order to choose a backtest input.

A reader captures a compressed slice once, verifies its SHA-256 and parses those exact bytes. Replacing a file between hash verification and CSV parsing cannot substitute a different observation under the recorded digest. Slice counts, instruments, time ranges and ordering must match their manifests. Partition and slice paths cannot escape the configured lake; symlinked partitions/manifests are refused before external writes. Provider symbols containing separators use distinct encoded partition names. Existing plain-symbol partitions, compressed payload hashes and legacy twelve-character slice filenames remain readable.

Writers reuse the existing cross-process state-file lock. Content and manifests are published by flushed atomic replacement. A failed manifest replacement preserves the prior readable snapshot and removes temporary files. A committed replacement followed by a lost acknowledgement can be retried without adding duplicate history. Concurrent threads and separate writer processes retain all committed slices. Complete digest filenames avoid relying on a shortened digest for newly written blobs. Content written before an interrupted manifest publication can remain unreferenced; it is not presented as a published observation.

These changes protect the existing downloader/import, session-service lake source, portfolio/market views and research consumers. They do not turn historical or synthetic data into live venue observations, adopt source-use rights, make a snapshot point-in-time safe for every learning feature, or establish a profitable strategy. The explicit legacy `verify=False` option skips the digest comparison only; path, identity, shape and market-value validation still applies, and the option does not qualify data for trading or research acceptance.

The evidence receipt retains eleven reproduced pre-fix failures, twenty-two focused cases, eight deliberate fault/control pairs and the full trading suite with a private PostgreSQL fixture and pinned DDS. A real, bounded HTTPS probe of one public Dukascopy historical hour returned HTTP 429; no vendor payload was admitted, and no provider integration was declared successful. Provider capability, terms, freshness, corrections, feed quotas and the remaining BE-008/015 requirements remain programme obligations.
