# BE-008: cTrader connection lifetime and recovery boundary

Status: candidate source component; complete BE-008 and native/provider acceptance remain open.

Owned socket tests reproduced seven failures on ac60c4d3: blocked readers outlived close, pending requests waited for timeout, disconnected sockets remained open, concurrent connection attempts created extra sockets/readers, retired readers and requests could close replacements, and TLS failure leaked the original TCP socket. Eight additional adapter probes reproduced reuse of authentication and contracts across replacement connections, wrong native authentication responses, mixed handshakes and unfenced refresh/recovery.

Transport readers and pending calls now belong to their original socket. State publication is serialized; reads retain their socket identity. Retirement detaches only that connection, wakes its original calls, shuts down and closes the socket, and joins its owned reader. Thread creation failure closes the socket and leaves no active connection. A reader that does not terminate after shutdown produces an explicit cleanup error. No platform process or unrelated worker is killed.

A process-local connection identity binds authentication and symbol discovery to the same native connection. It grants no authority. Application/account response types and the exact native account are verified before authentication is cached. A replacement reauthenticates and refreshes the native contract; failed refresh preserves diagnostic bytes while refusing their use. Previous quote caches are cleared. Queued quotes/depth retain their original connection identity, including events stashed by a waiter, and cannot seed a replacement connection. Original execution/error evidence remains available for reconciliation.

Cash/deal readers use the same scoped authentication without fetching market metadata or sharing the protective/order socket. Native disconnection still means an uncertain response/effect. No order is retried, no lost acknowledgement becomes a proven refusal, and final-send expiry checks remain in place. The deadline fixture's manually installed authenticated socket and contract now explicitly carry that fixture connection identity; every expiry, quantity, fill, uncertainty and native-write assertion is unchanged.

The original protocol peer fixture now owns and joins its serving thread. Its scoped pytest cleanup closes only sockets created by the current test and joins readers carrying those exact sockets, including imported cash/deal helpers. All new concurrency tests release and join their workers in cleanup.
