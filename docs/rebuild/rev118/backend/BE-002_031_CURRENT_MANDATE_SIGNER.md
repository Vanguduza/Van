# Current signer authority for standing trading policies

An admitted trading policy previously retained entry authority after the issuer's native key was retired. This candidate source binds admission to the exact verified public key and its original trust source, then checks current authority at risk evaluation and again at submission. Changing key material under an existing label, an unavailable registry, or revoking either the policy or its independent temperament signer prevents new risk. Equivalent PEM encodings and retirement of an unrelated key preserve valid authority.

The final submission boundary holds the same resolved state-file lock used by native key enrollment and revocation. A submission admitted before retirement may finish; retirement cannot acknowledge completion while that submission holds its guard. An approval issued earlier cannot start a later submission after retirement. Registry locks cover both signers in a deterministic order. This guards the synchronous router boundary; deferred provider execution still needs its own deadline and epoch enforcement.

Verified key bindings are private runtime handles attached after cryptographic verification. They do not alter signed policy content, risk hashes, dataclass serialization or historical receipts. Copying a policy with `dataclasses.replace` does not create a new admission. Reconfiguring a verifier does not change an already admitted policy's captured source or substitute newly loaded material for the public key that verified its signature.

Default runtime admission follows its current native registry. An explicitly injected archived trust view remains available for historical Paper research; it does not become live permission. In-memory issuer retirement is checked even for that explicit view. Standing-policy expiry, policy-content binding, physical account/cohort admission, kill switches and lease fences remain independent controls.

Session and account runtime observations expose `owner_policy_current`. Session entry permission becomes false after signer retirement. Protection and reduce-only exits remain available for existing exposure; retirement does not erase position state, withdraw stops, retry an uncertain order or grant a recovery action. Account-level risk evaluation and its final router use the same current authority checks.

The source receipt records the original failing counterexamples, corrected fixture failures, the full trading/recovery suite against an isolated PostgreSQL instance, real single-session and multi-instrument runtime controls, and nine deliberately introduced faults with restored source. Source checks do not issue native identity, provider execution, production, profitability, S24 or whole-unit acceptance. The original programme and BG-001 remain unchanged.
