# VATI Rev 3 — Research corpus (own research, 2026-09-16)

**Method.** Independent web research across trading-stack sources, practitioner forums, academic evidence and Zimbabwe-market sources, run from the build environment on 2026-09-16. Primary Zimbabwean sites (zse.co.zw, seczim.co.zw, rbz.co.zw, zsedirect.co.zw, ctrade.co.zw, vfex.exchange) and several others (wikipedia, arxiv, forexfactory, tradinghours, mystocks.africa, african-markets, mansamarkets, pindula, allafrica, equityaxis) were **egress-blocked** for direct fetch; their content reached this corpus only through search-engine summaries and is marked **[S]** (secondary via search). Facts read directly from a primary artefact are **[P]**. Every claim below carries its source; conflicting claims are kept side by side rather than resolved by preference.

Trust tiers follow VTIL (§9 of Rev 2): T1 official, T2 vendor/maintainer, T3 institutional research, T4 community.
