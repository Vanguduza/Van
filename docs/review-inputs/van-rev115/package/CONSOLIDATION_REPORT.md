## Acceptance needed for the architectural objective

A signed qualified operational policy must accept valid real/demo-bound evidence at its admitted grade; reconstruct a versioned state; select a different qualified method on a later eligible objective when the fixture's evidence supports that change; preserve that result across restart/replay; resist stale, duplicate, untrusted and synthetic-evidence substitution; protect every actual fill; and continue safe operations without research/Jev availability. Separately, a candidate must traverse real research, qualified evaluation, prospective evidence, independent review, authorized activation, monitoring and revocation with positive and negative results. Neither path is satisfied by a refusal-only stub.
