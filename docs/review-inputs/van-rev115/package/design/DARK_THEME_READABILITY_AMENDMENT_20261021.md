The images' #1D2127/#252930 surfaces and #D1D7E0/#AAAEB6 text are illustrative alternatives, not replacement tokens. `VAN_MATERIAL_TOKENS_REV2.json` remains the sole paired palette; the locked dark/light images and existing recipes remain preserved. ## Mandatory rendering rules

1. **Layered surfaces.** Use semantic canvas, navigation, surface, raised surface and inset roles to communicate hierarchy. Establish separation with surface luminance and, where needed, a visible edge. Do not flatten dialogs, inputs or actionable cards into the surrounding background. Home retains its spacious orb/Quick Hub composition; information density follows the existing screen contract.
2. **Readable text.** Use semantic primary, secondary and muted roles. Primary includes balances, quantities, order outcomes, instructions and critical notices. Secondary text still carries readable content and must meet its contrast target. Do not dim a whole parent container, apply low opacity to meaningful labels or place text over uncontrolled gradients/animation. A neutral backplate or layout adjustment must preserve legibility when the background varies.
3. **Measured contrast.** Ordinary text must meet at least 4.5:1 against the final composited background. A 3:1 threshold is permitted only for text documented as qualifying large text under WCAG sizing/weight rules; use 4.5:1 by default for Android financial text. Essential control boundaries, meaningful graphical indicators and focus indicators must meet at least 3:1 against applicable adjacent colours. For gradients, transparencies, dimmed dialogs and animated material, test the least favourable rendered region/state. Decorative edges are exempt only when they convey no necessary boundary or meaning. Truly inactive controls may use the accessibility exception, but any explanation of why an action is unavailable remains readable.
4. **Restrained depth.** Contact/ambient shadows may support the sculpted identity. Shadows alone must not identify a button, field, selected state or dialog boundary. Use explicit labels, shape, fill or an edge; distinguish pressed, focused, selected and disabled states. Keep charts and numerical labels crisp, and preserve visible focus outside clipping. Material effects must not compromise the existing 48dp minimum touch targets or reduced-motion behaviour.
5. **Explicit operational meaning.** Pair status colour with visible wording or a meaningful icon and accessible description. Profit/loss includes signed values and the applicable period; buy/sell direction is distinct from profit/loss. Connection/freshness, pending versus confirmed actions, partial fills, rejected/failed actions and unknown outcomes must remain distinguishable without colour. Display explicit live/simulated mode. Verified success, stale data and recovery cannot share an unlabelled green/red cue. Theme or animation changes never change authoritative state or execute/retry an action.
6. **Black and white are permitted.** Off-black/off-white are preferred defaults in the established material system, not absolute prohibitions. Pure black may be evaluated for OLED canvas regions; pure white may be used where contrast and the light-theme design warrant it. An OLED variant is optional, uses the existing preference surface if implemented, and needs its own full state/contrast checks; it is not a new mandatory screen or a new default. Honour system theme and explicit owner preference without resetting connection provisioning or account state.
7. **Whole-app and handset readability.** Apply these rules to all 234 contexts, sheets, dialogs, overlays and floating assistant surfaces, including loading, empty, error, offline, stale, unknown, permission-denied and recovery states where applicable. Validate dark and light themes, Android font/display scaling through the supported range (including 200% font scale), TalkBack, monochrome/colour-vision checks, low brightness and outdoor sunlight on the S24 Ultra. Reflow rather than crop; financial amounts, quantities and essential instructions remain readable. Recheck live theme switching during a pending action: retain state and focus, preserve the original operation identity and never resubmit it.
